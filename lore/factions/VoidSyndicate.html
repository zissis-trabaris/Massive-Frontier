Void Syndicate

Knives in the Dark

They do not hold territory; they hold throats. The Syndicate strikes from sensor-shadow and unregistered lanes, gutting a convoy before its captain finishes his coffee. Doctrine — Shadow Alpha-Strike: shield-piercing burst weapons and crippling EWAR on fragile, deniable hulls. Win in the first three seconds, or don't survive the fourth.

History

The Void Syndicate began as blockade runners during the Belt Uprising, flying ore and weapons through imperial pickets along lanes no chart recorded. When the uprising ended, they discovered that smuggling paid better than mining and that piracy paid better than smuggling.

The Syndicate holds no territory; it holds throats. Its cells strike from sensor-shadow, gut a convoy in seconds, and vanish down the unregistered lanes before anyone can respond. Its ships are fragile, deniable and armed to kill in the first volley, because a Syndicate crew that is still fighting in the fourth second has already lost.

On the Tide

The Syndicate trades with everyone, and nobody has ever proved that it doesn't trade with the Tide. It is almost certainly a lie. Almost.

“Every convoy we lose in the inner lanes, we lose to them. Every one we lose on the Rim, they warned us about first. Make of that what you will.”

— Frontier Command assessment, Fleet Admiral Selene Voss
