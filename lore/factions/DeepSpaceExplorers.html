Deep Space Explorers

Beyond the Last Star

The first to chart the black between the black, the Explorers fight the way they survey: from impossible distance, with instruments sharper than any blade. Their long-rail batteries strike before the enemy's sensors even resolve a target. Doctrine — Long-Range Precision: extreme range, projectile velocity and tracking backed by strong shields, mounted on light, sensor-laden frames. They hit you from yesterday.

History

The Deep Space Explorers are older than the Frontier. They were the pathfinder crews of the Long Dark, flying ahead of the arks to chart the black between stars, and it was their instruments that found Aurelia. They have never lost the habit of looking further than anyone else.

The Explorers fight the way they survey: from impossible range, with instruments sharper than any blade. They built the Farsight beacon line that first detected the Tide's return, and they hold the largest archive of precursor artifacts in the Frontier. They are also the power most frightened by what that archive says, though they are too disciplined to show it.

On the Tide

They saw it first and they have been studying it longest. They are not optimistic.
