Miner Rebellion

The Unbought

Forged in the choking dust of the outer belts by laborers who fed empires and starved in return. They weaponized the very tools that bled the asteroids dry. Doctrine — Industrial Kinetics: punishing armor-tearing slug weapons and rugged plating, paid for in scrap. Shields are an afterthought; survival is measured in how much you can bolt back on.

History

The Miner Rebellion was born in the Dust Famine, when the Empire cut the belt's rations and raised its quotas in the same decree. The miners who had fed the Ring for two centuries rose with the only weapons they had: mining lasers, ore launchers and drills. They turned out to be very good weapons.

They refused every offer of amnesty, won their freedom at the Siege of Bedrock and have held the outer belts ever since. The Rebellion is a federation of crews and rock-holds rather than a state, and it fights the way it mines: with heavy kinetic weapons that tear armor apart, ships built from scrap and a stubborn refusal to be bought. Every Rebellion warship is expected to be repaired with whatever is to hand.

On the Tide

The belt was the first thing the Tide stripped on the Rim. The Rebellion does not need to be told what is at stake.

“Undisciplined, undersupplied and very hard to kill. I would rather have a Rebellion crew at my back than most admirals.”

— Frontier Command assessment, Fleet Admiral Selene Voss
