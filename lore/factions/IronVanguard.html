Iron Vanguard

The Line Does Not Break

Where the Vanguard plants its banner, the war stops moving. Doctrine made of ceramite and stubbornness, they advance like a closing fist — slow, heavy, and utterly unkillable. Doctrine — Immovable Bastion: maximum armor and shields fronting slow, heavy-hitting weapons. Defensive and shield-restore specials make them the anvil every other faction breaks against.

History

The Iron Vanguard began as the Empire's Vanguard Legions, raised to garrison the Rim in case the thing that took the Cradle ever followed humanity to Aurelia. When the Throne recalled them to fight the civil war, they refused. They had been raised to hold the Rim, and they held it.

For a hundred and forty years the Vanguard's line did not break. Then came the Rim Burning, nineteen days against a swarm that did not tire, and on the twentieth day it broke. The Vanguard has never forgiven itself. It fights with maximum armor, maximum shields and slow, heavy guns, advancing like a closing fist, and it has sworn that the next time the Tide comes, it will not take a single step back.

On the Tide

The Tide broke their line once. Everything the Vanguard does is preparation for the rematch.

“My old legion. The finest soldiers in the Frontier and the worst at retreating. One of those will save you. The other will get you killed.”

— Frontier Command assessment, Fleet Admiral Selene Voss
