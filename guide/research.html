How research works

The Research Laboratory holds the module tech tree. Each node belongs to a category (Defense, Weapons, Special, Propulsion or Industry) and a tier (1 to 5). To start a node you need:

  1. every prerequisite node already researched;
  2. the player level for its tier;
  3. the resources, which are paid when the research starts.

What research unlocks

Each node unlocks module blueprints, usually a Ship, a Base and an Outpost version of the same module. You can't manufacture a module in the Forge until its node is researched.

Ship hulls are researched separately, in the Shipyard.

The research queue

Research runs one node at a time, first in, first out. Queue more to keep the lab busy. Queue size and research speed grow with your player level:
