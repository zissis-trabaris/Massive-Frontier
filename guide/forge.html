Manufacturing weapons, armor, shields and special modules.

“Research tells you what is possible. The Forge makes it real.”

— Fleet Admiral Selene Voss

Building modules

The Forge turns researched blueprints into modules you can fit. Pick a scope tab, then a type tab:

ScopeWhere the module goes
ShipFitted to your ships in Outfitting
BaseFitted to your base buildings' weapon, armor, shield and special slots
OutpostKept in your outpost inventory

Types are Weapon, Armor, Shield and Special. Each finished build adds one module to that scope's inventory. Fitting a module uses up one copy, and unfitting it gives the copy back.

In development

Outpost modules can be built, but nothing uses them yet.

Forge capacity

The number of modules you can build at the same time grows with your player level:
