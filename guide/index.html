Commander's Handbook

How Massive Frontier plays: screens, controls, economy, base, ships, fleets and combat.

This guide explains how Massive Frontier plays: the screens, the controls, the economy, your base, research, ships, fleets and combat. It describes the game as it is today. Features that are still being built are marked In development, so you always know what works now.

For the exact numbers behind every ship, module and building, see the Stat Reference. For the story of the Frontier, see the Lore.

Your first hour

  1. Launch the game through Steam. Your commander is created automatically the first time, with a home base in orbit of a planet. Welcome to the Frontier
  2. Open the Base View and press Collect All whenever your mines have filled up. Resources
  3. Build a Fusion Reactor for power, then more Mineral Mines. Upgrade the Command Bridge to unlock new buildings and more of the base grid. Your Homeworld
  4. Open the Shipyard and build your first ships. Fighters, Cutters and Corvettes need no research. Every finished ship also earns you player XP. The Shipyard
  5. Research modules in the Lab, manufacture them in the Forge, and fit them in Outfitting. The Tech Tree
  6. Put up to six ships in a fleet, deploy it, and right-click on the map to give orders. Command the Sector
