The HUD

The main HUD shows your commander (level and XP), your resources and coins, your fleets and a minimap. Every screen opens from a HUD button:

ButtonWhat it opens
Sector ViewThe map of the whole sector
Planet ViewA close view of your home planet and the objects around it
Base ViewYour home base: build, upgrade, move and collect
ShipyardResearch and build ship hulls
Research LaboratoryThe module tech tree
ForgeManufacture modules you have researched
Ship OutfittingFit modules to your ships
BookmarksSaved map locations

Moving around the map

InputAction
W A S D, or drag with the middle mouse buttonPan the camera
Mouse wheelZoom (towards the cursor)
Q / ERotate the camera
Page Up / Page DownRotate in steps
OReset the rotation
Left clickSelect a fleet, outpost, planet or system
Left-drag a boxSelect several of your own fleets
Right clickGive the selected fleet an order (on the map), or open a tile's menu (in the Base View)
EscQuit the game

Clicking the same spot again cycles through overlapping icons. When several things overlap, a fleet is picked before an outpost, an outpost before a planet, and a planet before a solar system.

Tip

Esc quits the game straight away; it doesn't open a menu.

Bookmarks

Save any location as a bookmark, then jump back to it from the Bookmarks list. Jumps work across sectors and select the bookmarked object for you.
