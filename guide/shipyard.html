Choosing a hull

The Shipyard lists hulls by faction, then class, then hull. There are ten classes, from the Fighter to the Dreadnought, and seven roles in each class. The role is the last word of every hull name:

Research, then build

Small hulls can be built straight away. Bigger hulls must be researched first. Research costs twice the hull's build cost and needs a minimum player level:

Shown for the Standard Issue (Unaligned) Striker of each class; every faction follows the same pattern.

Building a hull costs its build price, paid when the build starts. When the build finishes, you get the ship and player XP: the hull's XP value. Building ships is how your commander levels up.

Queues and bay space

Your shipyard has limited room. Right now every commander has:

LimitValue
Ships in your bay (built + building)5
Ship builds at once1

Queued items count against those limits, and finished ships count against your bay. A hull tile shows why you can't build it: Research Required, Requires Level, Bay Full, Build Queue Full, Research Queue Full or Insufficient Resources.
