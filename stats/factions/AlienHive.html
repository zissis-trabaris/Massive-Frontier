Alien Hive

Not a fleet — a single will wearing ten thousand bodies. The Hive does not build ships; it grows them, sealing wounds with living resin and dissolving prey in chain-spreading acid. Doctrine — Living Swarm: chaining, spreading bio-weapons and self-regenerating armor that knits closed mid-battle. There is only the tide, and the tide is always rising.
