The layout

Your base is a square grid of tiles (pads) with the Command Bridge at its centre. Every tile has a type, and a tile only takes buildings of its own type: Turret tiles hold turrets, Production tiles hold mines and extractors, and so on. ANY tiles take any building. Hover a tile on the map to see its type and the Bridge level that unlocks it; use the buttons to see the base at each level. Locked tiles are still drawn on your deck, dimmed, until your Bridge reaches their level.

How the base grows

You start with the 25 tiles closest to the Bridge. Each Command Bridge upgrade unlocks a new ring of tiles around the old edge, and new building types become available. The costs below are for upgrading the Bridge to that level.

Building, moving and changing tiles in the Base View

Building on a tile

  1. Open the Base View from the HUD.
  2. Pick a building from the construction list. Its ghost follows your cursor over the deck.
  3. The ghost turns green over a tile that can take it (an unlocked, free tile of the building's type, or an ANY tile) and red anywhere else. Use the mouse wheel to rotate it in 15° steps.
  4. Click to place it. It joins your build queue and occupies the tile at once while it is built.

Free, unlocked tiles show their type on the deck ("TURRET", "REPAIR BAY", "ANY"...). If the construction list says "No free Turret pad" (or any other type), you have run out of tiles of that type. Free one up, or re-type a tile as described below.

Moving, rotating and demolishing

  • Select a building and use Move / Rotate in its inspector to move it to another tile. Moving is free and instant. The new tile must be unlocked, free and of the right type (or ANY), and the building must not be under construction or upgrading. It can still be rotated in place while busy.
  • Demolish removes a building instantly and frees its tile. You get no refund, and any modules fitted to it are lost. The Command Bridge can't be demolished.
  • The Command Bridge can be moved, but only onto an ANY tile.

Changing a tile's type

  1. Make sure the tile is unlocked: your Command Bridge level must be at least the tile's unlock level (see the map above).
  2. Make sure the tile is empty. Move or demolish whatever stands on it first. A building still under construction also counts as occupying the tile.
  3. Select the tile in the Base View to open the Change Tile Type menu.
  4. Choose the new type. The menu shows the Minerals cost. The change is instant and permanent until you re-type the tile again.
  • Any tile can become any of: Production, Storage, Utility, Repair Bay, Turret, Defense, Shield, Screen, Barricade or Combat.
  • Tiles cannot be turned into ANY or Building tiles. An ANY tile you re-type is gone for good, so keep your ANY tiles for buildings you move around often.
  • The Command Bridge's own centre tile can't be re-typed.
  • The new type must differ from the current one, and you must be able to pay the cost.
